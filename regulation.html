Regulation & Compliance

We're building Meridian the right way — regulation comes before revenue. Our licence applications are in progress and compliance infrastructure is being built in parallel with our platform.

Licensing Status

We're pursuing regulatory approval in jurisdictions chosen for their credibility and operational fit

Mauritius — FSC

Application In Progress

Investment Dealer licence application filed with the Financial Services Commission of Mauritius. This jurisdiction offers a robust regulatory framework recognised internationally, with clear capital requirements and investor protection standards.

Dubai — DIFC

Application In Progress

Category 3C licence application in preparation with the Dubai International Financial Centre. Our headquarters are already established in DIFC, and we're working closely with local counsel to meet all requirements.

Additional Jurisdictions

Planned — 2027

We're evaluating additional jurisdictions for our next phase of expansion. Our regulatory strategy prioritises credible, well-regarded authorities that provide our clients with genuine legal protection and recourse.

Our Approach

Compliance By Design

Rather than launching first and dealing with compliance later, we're building our regulatory infrastructure alongside our technology. Our compliance team has been part of the founding team since day one.

Client Protection — From Day One

We're implementing institutional-grade protections before we onboard a single live client

Segregated Funds

All client deposits will be held in segregated accounts at tier-1 banking institutions, completely separate from company operating capital.

Negative Balance Protection

Your account balance cannot go below zero. We guarantee negative balance protection — you'll never owe funds beyond your deposit.

Data Encryption

All client data and transactions are protected with AES-256 encryption, 2FA authentication, and strict access controls across our entire infrastructure.

Independent Audits

We've engaged an independent audit firm from pre-launch. Financial statements and operational processes will be verified annually and made available to regulators.

AML & KYC Compliance

Industry-standard procedures to prevent financial crime — built in, not bolted on

KYC Verification

Comprehensive identity verification and customer profiling. We verify personal information, source of funds, and trading experience to ensure proper client categorisation and suitability.

Anti-Money Laundering

AML procedures aligned with FATF recommendations. Continuous monitoring for suspicious activity and comprehensive sanctions screening against global watchlists.

Transaction Monitoring

Real-time monitoring of all account activity. Automated systems detect unusual patterns and flag suspicious transactions for review by our compliance team.

Data Protection

Full GDPR compliance with strict data protection standards. Client data is encrypted at rest and in transit, with access limited to authorised personnel only.

Operational Transparency

How we plan to execute trades — no conflicts of interest, no hidden markups

STP Execution

Straight-Through Processing model — all orders route directly to our liquidity providers. No manual intervention, no dealing desk manipulation.

No Dealing Desk

Direct market access through our LP network. We earn on spreads and commissions, not by trading against you. Our interests are aligned with yours.

Execution Reporting

We will publish execution statistics — fill rates, slippage data, and order flow — so clients can independently verify the quality of our execution.

Open Roadmap

Our regulatory and product roadmap is shared openly. We believe transparency about where we are — and where we're going — builds more trust than marketing claims.
